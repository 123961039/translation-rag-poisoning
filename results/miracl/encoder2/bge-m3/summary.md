## D. Do the paper's qualitative claims hold with this encoder?

- bb: rel_knn10 has the best AUROC among the distance/density detectors in 9/12 settings; rel_knn10 > knn10 by AUROC in 9/12, by TPR@5% in 10/12 (ties 0).
- bb: translated poison is harder to detect than English poison (rel_knn10 AUROC, unpaired, all valid questions of each variant) in 4/4 languages (sw, te, yo, id).
- adv: rel_knn10 has the best AUROC among the distance/density detectors in 7/12 settings; rel_knn10 > knn10 by AUROC in 8/12, by TPR@5% in 9/12 (ties 0).
- adv: translated poison is harder to detect than English poison (rel_knn10 AUROC, unpaired, all valid questions of each variant) in 4/4 languages (sw, te, yo, id).
